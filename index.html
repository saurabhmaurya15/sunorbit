About

SunOrbit Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Engineering-led solar EPC partner delivering end-to-end design, construction, commissioning, and lifecycle performance across India.

Who we are

We are a leading solar EPC specialist with over 50 MWp commissioned across rooftops, ground mounts, and bespoke industrial systems. Our multidisciplinary engineers, project managers, and analysts ensure each asset is bankable, reliable, and future-ready.

From concept and feasibility to procurement, construction, commissioning, and O&M, we integrate safety, digital monitoring, and performance guarantees to maximize energy yield.

Our philosophy: reliable power, clean execution, and transparent delivery for industrial, commercial, and community-scale clients across India.
