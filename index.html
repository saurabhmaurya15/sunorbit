About SunOrbit Solutions

A trusted solar EPC partner delivering reliable, bankable, and future-ready clean energy projects across India.

Who We Are

SunOrbit Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a leading solar EPC company with 50+ MWp of installations across India. We deliver end-to-end solar solutions covering design, detailed engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, operations, and maintenance.

Our teams combine field experience with data-driven decision-making to ensure high-performing assets. From rooftop systems to utility-scale plants, we focus on reliability, safety, and lifecycle performance.

We align with our customers’ sustainability goals, ensuring every project is bankable, scalable, and simple to operate for decades.
